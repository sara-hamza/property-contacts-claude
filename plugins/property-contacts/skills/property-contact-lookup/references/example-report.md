## Recommended first contact
1. **Quintessential** on (03) 9914 2214, or the enquiry form on 100kws.com.au. Ask for the **property / facilities manager for 100 King William St, Adelaide**. As owner, Quintessential can authorise or route site-access requests.
2. **Backup:** the **onsite building management or concierge** at the refurbished ground-floor lobby. Ask in person or have a tenant ask. For comms-room or riser access, the facilities manager is the person who signs off.
3. Leasing agents (Colliers/JLL) know the owner's team but don't control site access. Use them only if the first two don't respond.

## Gaps and how to confirm
- **Facilities / property manager name and direct line:** ask Quintessential, or check the lobby directory or the building's tenant-handbook signage.
- **Full tenant list:** check the lobby directory board, or a paid tenancy database (e.g. CoStar / Cordell). Levels 4 and 11 had vacant suites in late 2024 [12], so the occupancy has probably changed.
- **Mouldens:** confirm it is still at Level 14. It may be stale, as DW Fox Tucker now lists that level.
- **Quintessential's ABN:** ABN Lookup returned several similar entities, none clearly the building owner, so none has been assigned. Get it from Quintessential's terms or invoice.
- **AGL / CBA local site contacts:** these aren't published. Go through each company's corporate property or facilities team.
