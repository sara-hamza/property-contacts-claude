# Strata / owners corporation registers by state

Use the free check first. Recommend the paid search to the user, with its name; never buy it yourself. Register names change, so confirm the current one before citing it, and never claim a result you haven't seen.

| State | Term | Free check | Paid / definitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSW | Strata scheme / owners corporation | NSW Fair Trading **Strata Hub** public search (scheme, managing agent); StrataChecks mirrors it | NSW LRS strata plan / title search |
| VIC | Owners corporation | Business Licensing Authority / Consumer Affairs Victoria **public register of owners corporation managers**: confirms a named manager is registered and gives its business contact | Landata owners corporation search / certificate |
| QLD | Body corporate | Body corporate manager's own portfolio pages | Titles Queensland community management statement (CMS) search |
| SA | Strata or community corporation | Manager's portfolio pages | Land Services SA strata / community plan search |
| WA, TAS, ACT, NT | Strata company / body corporate | Manager's portfolio pages; check whether the state licenses managers and publishes a register | State land titles office strata plan search |

Tips
- Plan numbers appear in sale and rental listings ("SP12345", "PS437659", "OC1") and on building-insight pages. Treat a single source as Medium until it's confirmed.
- One complex can have several owners corporations, e.g. a retail OC and a residential OC, or a stratum over the whole site. Ask the manager.
- Search for the building's own resident or owner portal, e.g. "<building> residents", "<building> living", "<building> induction". These often name the operations manager and the owners corporation manager.
