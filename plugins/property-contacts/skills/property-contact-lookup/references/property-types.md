# Choosing the path by property type

| Type | Signs | Who controls access | Route | Traps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Residential strata / apartments** | "SP"/"PS"/"OC" plan numbers, unit numbers in listings, concierge | Owners corporation (OC) manager, or the onsite building manager they appoint | Building portal → OC manager (state register) → concierge as backup | Old listings name previous managers; the building's own site wins |
| **Single-owner commercial tower** | One owner in press releases, leasing campaign, building marketing site | Owner / asset manager → their facilities manager | Owner's head office or "contact" form → ask for property / facilities manager | Marketing sites list **leasing agents only**; agents don't control access |
| **Mixed-use precinct** | Mall plus towers; unit prefixes E/W, A/B; several street addresses | A different manager for each part | Split by address: retail centre management; each tower's OC | One street number ≠ one manager; look for a Building Management Statement |
| **Retail centre** | Centre website, "Centre Management" phone | Centre management (owner's manager) | Official centre site → owner | Directory pages are often broken; use the centre map |
| **Industrial / business park** | Estate name, multiple warehouses | Owner or estate manager; sometimes an owners corporation | Estate site → owner → leasing agent | Tenants change often; confirm each is still operating |
| **Greenfield lot / new estate** | "Lot 123", estate name, sales centre | Developer's project team; council for verges and streets | Developer's state office (civil works) → sales centre → builders' portal → council | Lot numbers aren't street numbers; geocoders return the suburb; **never research the lot purchaser** |
| **Single private dwelling** | House, no businesses, no strata | Out of scope | Stop. Point to council and utility routes | Don't identify residents or owners |

**Site security by type** (Part C)
- **Commercial towers:** usually a security control room or desk on the ground floor; contractor sign-in and induction through the facilities manager.
- **Residential strata:** concierge or building manager; dock bookings and contractor inductions are often on the resident portal.
- **Retail centres:** centre management plus a security control room; after-hours works need a permit from centre management.
- **Industrial parks:** often patrol-based, with no desk; access goes through the owner or estate manager.
- **Greenfield:** the developer's site team or head contractor runs the gate and site inductions.

**Split addresses** (number ranges like 96–100, corner sites): tenants may use either number, so search all variants.
