## Recommended first contact
- **Retail or podium works (shops, loading dock, retail risers):** Central Park Mall Centre Management, +61 2 8123 4555.
- **Residential towers (risers, comms rooms, apartment access):** Dynamic Property Services, (02) 9267 6334, enquiries@dynamicproperty.com.au. Quote **SP87881** for 2 Chippendale Way. Ask them:
  - who the onsite building manager is;
  - whether SP87881 covers the whole West tower, or whether there is a building management committee or stratum plan above it;
  - who manages the East tower at 3 Carlton St.
- **Backup:** the 24-hour concierge at the West tower entrance on Chippendale Way.

## Gaps and how to confirm
- **East tower (3 Carlton St) strata plan and manager:** search NSW Strata Hub by address (free, interactive), or ask Dynamic Property Services.
- **Whether a building management committee or stratum covers the whole precinct:** large mixed-use NSW sites often have a Building Management Statement. Ask the strata manager or the centre management.
- **Onsite building manager company and phone:** not published. Ask the strata manager.
- **Current retail owner:** check NSW LRS title (paid) or recent press.
- **Full tenant list, levels and ABNs:** the mall directory page returned 404. Use the in-centre directory or the mall's map.
